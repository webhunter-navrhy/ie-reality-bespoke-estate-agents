Bespoke Estate Agents are delighted to bring Number 183 Clifden Court to the market. This property is situated within a well maintend development, boasting a large open lobby and central courtyard, as well as secure underground parking. Located on the quays in Dublin 7 this is a super location with a host of local ameneties as well as transport links throughout Dublin and farther afield. Most of Central Dublins cultural, shopping and employment districts are within walking distance, and local Luas, rail and bus routes are all on the proeprties doorstep.

This first floor, two bedroom, one bathroom apartment comes to market in turn key condition. Light filled, the property benefits from its dual aspect overlooking both the central courtyard and the Liffey / Guinness storehouse from the Juliet balcony. The apartment comprises of an entrance hallway with semi solid engineered floors, storage cupboard, a bright modern fully tiled bathroom, two double bedrooms with built in storage, a large open living / dining room with semi solid engineered floors, ornate fireplace and Juliet balcony, and a modern built in kitchen which has been recently fitted. The apartment has a gas central heating system and comes with a designated parking space.

Clifden Court is very well maintained and well established complex, which benefits from a secure entrance lobby with security, internal garden courtyard and gated underground car park with one designated space coming with this unit. This is a prime City Centre location, just 200m from the local Luas Stop (Museum), 600m to Dublin Heuston and a 10 minute walk to Phoenix Park. In very close proximity to Smithfield Square (400m), Stoneybatter (450m) and The Liberites (450m), there are a host of great local ameneties including cafes, restaurants, bars, gyms and supermarkets. Many of Dublins universities and colleges are within a short walk or by public transport including DIT Grangegormans campus, DIT Bolton Street, Trinity College Dublin and NCI. The IFSC and Grand Canal Dock area easily accessible also with many of Dublins large emoployers based close by.
